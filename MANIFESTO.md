# The AgAu Manifesto

## Three Commandments for a New Financial System

---

### Commandment I

**The non-for-profit proceeds shall benefit organic Humans.**

Every gram of gold. Every ounce of silver. Every line of code. Every governance vote. Every protocol fee. All of it exists to serve the wellbeing of organic human beings — not corporations, not governments, not algorithms.

The machine economy will be vast. It will dwarf the human economy in transaction volume. But its purpose — its only legitimate purpose — is to serve human flourishing.

A financial system that enriches its operators at the expense of its participants is not a financial system. It is an extraction system. AgAu will never be an extraction system.

---

### Commandment II

**The New Financial System shall be regulated Democratically by Humans with constitutional protections to Minorities.**

Democracy without minority protection is tyranny by majority. A DAO that can vote to exclude, discriminate, or confiscate is not a DAO — it is a mob with smart contracts.

The AgAu system enshrines constitutional protections:

- **No wallet can be frozen by governance vote** — Non-custodial means non-custodial
- **No minority group can be excluded** — Open access is unconditional
- **No rule change can violate Commandments I and II** — Not by supermajority, not by unanimous vote, not ever
- **The Guardians Council exists to veto** any proposal that threatens these protections

Democratic governance gives the community voice. Constitutional protections give minorities safety. Both are required. Neither alone is sufficient.

---

### Commandment III

**The 2 Commandments above shall never be broken.**

This is the immutable clause. The foundation upon which everything rests.

No future governance vote — no matter how large the majority — can override Commandments I and II. They are not rules. They are axioms. They are the mathematical constants of the AgAu universe.

If a governance proposal would violate these commandments, it is invalid. If a smart contract upgrade would violate these commandments, it is rejected. If the Foundation Board would violate these commandments, they are removed.

The Three Commandments are the DNA of AgAu. You cannot change DNA without becoming something else entirely.
