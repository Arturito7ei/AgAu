## What is AgAu?

**Ag** (Silver, atomic number 47) + **Au** (Gold, atomic number 79) = **AgAu**

AgAu.io was founded in 2018 by **Thierry Arys Ruiz** in Zug, Switzerland (Crypto Valley). It pioneered the tokenisation of physical gold and silver on Ethereum:

- **FINMA-compatible** — Swiss regulators accepted asset-backed precious metals tokens
- **1:1 full reserve** — Every token backed by 1 gram of LBMA-quality metal in a Swiss vault
- **Redeemable** — Token holders can redeem for physical bars
- **Proven** — Secured seed funding from commodity trading veterans including Nicolas Chikhani, former CEO of Arab Bank
